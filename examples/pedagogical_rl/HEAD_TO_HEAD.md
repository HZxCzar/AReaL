# Head-to-head protocol

Train each teacher only with its own method, then cross-evaluate the frozen
adapters under both complete environments:

| trained teacher | our 0901 protocol | PedagogicalRL protocol |
| --- | --- | --- |
| our all-ID teacher | existing tutor full-matrix evaluator | `configs/comparison/eval_8gpu.yaml` |
| PedagogicalRL teacher | `run_tutor_protocol_eval.sh` | `configs/comparison/eval_8gpu.yaml` |

PedagogicalRL training has no preference and no pre-solve. Our native evaluation
uses our prompts, unverified evaluation-time pre-solve, masked leak/preference
turns, scripted responses, and retest. PedagogicalRL evaluation uses its native
prompts and GUIDED/ATTEMPTED classroom; its native whole-dialogue leak decision
is reported both raw and with leaked improvements replaced by zero. Preference
students are an evaluation-only layer in both columns.

All comparison runs are self-hosted: Qwen3-1.7B is a local SGLang student and
the Qwen3-8B auxiliary/judges are generated by the rollout base model with LoRA
disabled. No web inference endpoint is part of either arm.

The only shared output-interface adaptation is
`<reasoning>...</reasoning><output>...</output>` or
`<reasoning>...</reasoning><end></end>`, so either checkpoint can enter either
environment without an interface-induced format failure. See `README.md` for
the exact training contract and commands.
